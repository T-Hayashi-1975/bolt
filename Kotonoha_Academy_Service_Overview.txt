3. Why Kotonoha Is Different / 差別化要因
----------------------------------------------------------------

1. A native specialist, not a generalist tutor.
   Our founder is a Japanese national, formally trained in
   education, with direct experience inside Japan's own
   university entrance examination culture — widely regarded
   as the most rigorous in the world. The standards we bring
   to a VCE classroom are calibrated to that benchmark.

2. One-to-one. Always.
   No group classes. No rotating tutors. Every session is
   delivered personally, in private, by your child's dedicated
   mentor. This is not a franchise. This is a studio.

3. Built for the exam — every word of it.
   The VCE Japanese examination rewards precision: vocabulary
   lists, kanji selection, listening discrimination, and the
   unforgiving rubric of the oral assessment. We have studied
   each component in depth. Every lesson is engineered to
   convert effort into marks.

4. Limited enrolment.
   We accept a small number of students each academic year.
   Once the cohort is full, the waiting list opens for the
   following year. This is not a marketing posture — it is a
   quality requirement.

   Concentrated preparation for the written and oral
   examinations.

We do not discount, and we do not run trial promotions.
We do, however, offer a complimentary 30-minute consultation
to determine whether Kotonoha is the right fit for your child.
